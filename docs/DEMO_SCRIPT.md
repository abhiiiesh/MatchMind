### [0:00 – 0:15] The Hook & Problem Statement
* **Visual:** Close-up of the MatchMind dashboard with the pulsing green Premier League AI badge, glowing pitch visualizer, and live commentary feed.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Every weekend, 1.87 billion fans watch the Premier League. But while modern clubs have access to multi-million dollar data analytics, fans and broadcasters are left with basic stats and generic commentary. Today, we're changing that. Welcome to **MatchMind** — the explainable, multi-agent football intelligence engine powered by Microsoft Azure."*

---

### [0:15 – 0:45] The Replay Engine & "Why It Matters" Explainability
* **Visual:** Presenter clicks the Timeline Scrubber highlight pin at minute 67: Gabriel Martinelli's goal against Liverpool. Pitch vectors animate, metrics update, and the Explainability Card appears.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"MatchMind doesn't just tell you WHAT happened — our Narrative Agent explains **WHY it matters**. When Martinelli scores in minute 67, MatchMind instantly classifies the story arc as a 'High-Leverage Defensive Collapse'. It calculates the 0.74 xG chance and correlates it with Van Dijk and Alisson's spatial collision, pulling historical rivalry context from our Azure Cosmos DB RAG engine."*

---

### [0:45 – 1:10] Persona Personalization & Azure AI Speech Neural Audio
* **Visual:** Presenter clicks `🔬 Tactical Analyst` $\to$ text shifts to deep PPDA and spatial terminology. Then clicks `🎉 Casual Fan` $\to$ text turns into high-energy celebratory calls. Then clicks `</> SSML` on the audio bar, showing the Azure SSML markup, and hits play to hear `en-GB-RyanNeural` and `en-GB-AlfieNeural`. Then switches language to `🇪🇸 Español`.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Football isn't one-size-fits-all. With one click, MatchMind rewrites the entire commentary across four audience personas: Tactical Analyst, Casual Fan, Broadcast Call, and Accessible Audio Description. Even better: our Azure AI Speech engine synthesizes real-time neural commentary with dynamic SSML tags — automatically inflecting pitch and rate during high-leverage goals across 6 global languages."*

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] Tactical Spatial Intelligence (Heatmaps & Pass Networks)
* **Visual:** Presenter clicks `🔥 Heatmap` on the pitch visualizer $\to$ smooth Gaussian contours appear. Clicks `Bukayo Saka` $\to$ heatmap isolates Saka's right-wing flank. Clicks `🕸️ Pass Network` $\to$ glowing cyan passing lines connect the 11 player centroids. Clicks `🛡️ Pressing` $\to$ pitch thirds demarcate high-press zones (37.3%).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"On the pitch, MatchMind provides live spatial intelligence. We can toggle 2D Gaussian smoothed touch heatmaps, drill down into individual player flank heatmaps, inspect real-time passing networks with player centroid positions, and analyze defensive pressing zones in the attacking third — all computed on the fly from StatsBomb coordinates."*

---

### [1:35 – 1:50] The 7-Agent Architecture & Fact-Checking Guardrail
* **Visual:** Camera pans down to the persistent Multi-Agent Status Bar showing all 7 agents glowing green with microsecond latencies. Presenter quickly shows the transparent OBS Broadcast Overlay running in a browser tab.
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Under the hood, a cluster of seven specialized micro-agents orchestrates the entire pipeline in under 200 microseconds — from spatial ingestion and mathematical metrics to RAG retrieval and our deterministic Fact-Checking Agent, which mathematically prevents hallucinated scorelines before broadcasting to OBS Studio or web clients."*

---

### [1:50 – 2:00] Conclusion & Real-World Impact
* **Visual:** Full dashboard wide shot, showing live audio equalizer pulsating and the Premier League fixture selector. Text overlay: *"Built with Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Speech, Cosmos DB & Container Apps."*
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Built natively for Microsoft Azure Container Apps with full Bicep IaC, MatchMind bridges the gap between elite tactical data and global fan storytelling.*  
  > *1.87 billion fans. 190 countries. One intelligence layer. Thank you!"*
